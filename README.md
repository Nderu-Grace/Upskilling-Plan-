<section class="sec" id="glossary">
  <div class="sh"><span class="sn">01</span><h2 class="st">Glossary — Every Acronym Decoded</h2></div>
  <p class="ss">Learn these cold before anything else. You will encounter every one of these in your Google Ads account daily.</p>
  <div class="gg">
    <div class="gc"><div class="ga ca">CPC</div><div class="gf">Cost Per Click</div><div class="gd">What you pay each time someone clicks your ad. Formula: spend ÷ clicks. The fundamental bidding unit. Moringa's problem: $0.01 max CPC can't win auctions.</div></div>
    <div class="gc"><div class="ga cg">CPA</div><div class="gf">Cost Per Acquisition</div><div class="gd">What you pay for one conversion. Formula: spend ÷ conversions. Moringa's current CPA: $42–$103. Target: $15–$30. The primary efficiency metric for lead-gen.</div></div>
    <div class="gc"><div class="ga ct">CPL</div><div class="gf">Cost Per Lead</div><div class="gd">Same as CPA but specifically for leads — form fills, sign-ups, phone calls. Moringa's most important metric since course leads are the goal.</div></div>
    <div class="gc"><div class="ga cc">CPM</div><div class="gf">Cost Per 1,000 Impressions</div><div class="gd">What you pay per 1,000 ad views. Used in Display and Demand Gen when the goal is brand awareness rather than direct clicks.</div></div>
    <div class="gc"><div class="ga cgr">CTR</div><div class="gf">Click-Through Rate</div><div class="gd">% of people who saw your ad and clicked. Formula: clicks ÷ impressions × 100. Strong CTR signals relevance — one of three Quality Score components.</div></div>
    <div class="gc"><div class="ga ca">CVR</div><div class="gf">Conversion Rate</div><div class="gd">% of clicks that become a lead or sale. Formula: conversions ÷ clicks × 100. Education benchmark: 2–5%. Moringa is at 0.47–1.0%.</div></div>
    <div class="gc"><div class="ga cg">ROAS</div><div class="gf">Return On Ad Spend</div><div class="gd">Revenue per dollar spent. $5 ROAS = every $1 returned $5. More relevant for e-commerce with variable order values than fixed-price course leads.</div></div>
    <div class="gc"><div class="ga ct">QS</div><div class="gf">Quality Score</div><div class="gd">Google's 1–10 score per keyword based on: Expected CTR + Ad Relevance + Landing Page Experience. Higher QS = lower CPC + better position. Moringa best: QS 8.</div></div>
    <div class="gc"><div class="ga cc">IS</div><div class="gf">Impression Share</div><div class="gd">% of eligible auctions where your ad showed. Moringa: 10–14%, meaning 86% of opportunities lost. Root cause: $0.01 max CPC can't beat Coursera bidding $1–$3.</div></div>
    <div class="gc"><div class="ga cgr">RSA</div><div class="gf">Responsive Search Ad</div><div class="gd">Standard Search ad format. Supply up to 15 headlines + 4 descriptions. Google tests combinations. Strength rated: Average / Good / Excellent.</div></div>
    <div class="gc"><div class="ga ca">tCPA</div><div class="gf">Target CPA Bidding</div><div class="gd">Smart bidding — you set your target cost per conversion, Google adjusts bids automatically. Requires 30+ conversions before it works properly.</div></div>
    <div class="gc"><div class="ga cg">tROAS</div><div class="gf">Target ROAS Bidding</div><div class="gd">You set your target return; Google bids to hit it. More useful when revenue per conversion varies. Less relevant for a fixed-price course like Moringa's.</div></div>
    <div class="gc"><div class="ga ct">PMax</div><div class="gf">Performance Max</div><div class="gd">Fully automated campaign across all Google channels — Search, Display, YouTube, Gmail, Maps, Discover — from a single campaign. Needs 30+ conversions first.</div></div>
    <div class="gc"><div class="ga cc">Demand Gen</div><div class="gf">Demand Generation</div><div class="gd">Visual/video campaign on YouTube, Shorts, Gmail, Discover. Replaced Video Action Campaigns in 2025. Creates demand before people search.</div></div>
    <div class="gc"><div class="ga cgr">GTM</div><div class="gf">Google Tag Manager</div><div class="gd">Free tool for adding tracking scripts without code. Critical for firing conversion events. Moringa has a broken GTM trigger URL — a developer must fix it.</div></div>
    <div class="gc"><div class="ga ca">GA4</div><div class="gf">Google Analytics 4</div><div class="gd">Google's analytics platform. When linked to Ads, it shares conversion data. Broken GA4 event = Google's AI optimises completely blind.</div></div>
    <div class="gc"><div class="ga cg">SQR</div><div class="gf">Search Query Report</div><div class="gd">Shows the actual words people typed that triggered your ads. Your most important weekly task — find irrelevant queries, add as negatives, stop budget waste.</div></div>
    <div class="gc"><div class="ga cp">SERP</div><div class="gf">Search Engine Results Page</div><div class="gd">The page Google shows after a search. Your Search ads appear at the top and bottom, above organic results.</div></div>
  </div>
</section>

  <div class="cl2 b">Search must always come first and hold the largest budget share until CPA is proven. <strong>Display, Demand Gen, and PMax are amplifiers — not shortcuts or replacements.</strong></div>

<section class="sec" id="analogy">
  <div class="sh"><span class="sn">03</span><h2 class="st">The Restaurant Analogy</h2></div>
  <p class="ss">Each campaign type is a stage in growing a restaurant. You don't run a city-wide TV commercial before proving the kitchen works.</p>
  <div class="ac2 s"><div class="albl">Phase 1 — Search</div><h3>Your restaurant opens its front door</h3><p>Search is <strong>serving customers already standing outside with money, reading your menu.</strong> They typed "AI marketing course Nairobi" — they have intent and money, they just need to choose you. Prove the offer converts here first before spending on any other channel.</p></div>
  <div class="ac2 d"><div class="albl">Phase 2 — Display</div><h3>A loyalty card for guests who peeked in and walked out</h3><p>Display is <strong>the loyalty card for someone who came in, looked at the menu, then left without ordering.</strong> Retargeting follows those people across the web. Someone visited the AI Agents landing page and left — Display brings them back for a fraction of re-acquisition cost.</p></div>
  <div class="ac2 dg"><div class="albl">Phase 3 — Demand Gen</div><h3>A viral short video creating hunger before people knew they were hungry</h3><p>Demand Gen <strong>creates hunger in people not yet thinking about eating.</strong> They're scrolling YouTube Shorts — a 30-second story of a career transformation after your AI course appears. They didn't search for you. Now they will. This builds the pipeline that converts through Search.</p></div>
  <div class="ac2 pm"><div class="albl">Phase 4 — Performance Max</div><h3>A world-class maître d' running the whole operation — but needing training first</h3><p>PMax is <strong>giving Google full control of your restaurant, kitchen, and delivery service.</strong> Powerful — but only after training. Google needs 30+ conversions to learn your best customers. Before that, it spends blindly across all channels with no signal.</p></div>
  <div class="cl2 g"><strong>Sequencing rule:</strong> Search → Display → Demand Gen → PMax. Each requires the previous one working with real data. <strong>Sequencing rule: Search first, always. Fix what's broken before adding new campaign types.</strong></div>
</section>

  <div class="sh"><span class="sn">06</span><h2 class="st">Plateau Playbook — When Campaigns Stop Growing</h2></div>
  <p class="ss">A plateaued campaign is one that was working but has stalled. There are six distinct plateau types, each requiring a different fix. Identify which one you have before taking action.</p>
  <div class="cl2 co"><strong>The #1 mistake when hitting a plateau:</strong> changing your bidding strategy. Switching from tCPA to Maximize Conversions (or vice versa) resets the machine learning and starts a new 4-week learning period. Always identify the plateau type first and use the targeted fix below.</div>
  <div class="plat-grid">
    <div class="plat-card">
      <div class="plat-hd"><div class="plat-type" style="color:var(--re)">Plateau Type 1</div><div class="plat-title">Volume Plateau — Impressions/Clicks Declining</div></div>
      <div class="plat-body">
        <p><strong>Symptoms:</strong> Impressions dropping, IS falling, clicks/leads declining despite same budget.</p>
        <p><strong>Causes:</strong> Seasonal demand drop, impression share lost to budget, keyword match too narrow.</p>
        <ul>
          <li>Check IS Lost to Budget vs IS Lost to Rank — these have different fixes</li>
          <li>Add new keyword themes from SQR converting queries</li>
          <li>Expand phrase match on best performing ad groups</li>
          <li>Add adjacent long-tail keywords for AI Agents / Gen AI topics</li>
          <li>Add Display retargeting to stay visible while Search costs rise</li>
        </ul>
        <div class="plat-signal"><span class="psig" style="background:var(--rb);color:var(--re)">Impressions ↓</span><span class="psig" style="background:var(--rb);color:var(--re)">IS% ↓</span><span class="psig" style="background:var(--gb);color:var(--go)">CPA holding</span></div>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="plat-card">
      <div class="plat-hd"><div class="plat-type" style="color:var(--co)">Plateau Type 2</div><div class="plat-title">CPA Plateau — Leads Coming but Too Expensive</div></div>
      <div class="plat-body">
        <p><strong>Symptoms:</strong> Conversions happening but CPA is above target and not improving.</p>
        <p><strong>Causes:</strong> Budget spread too thin across bad keywords, landing page not converting.</p>
        <ul>
          <li>Run SQR audit — find the queries consuming budget without converting</li>
          <li>Pause keywords with 2x target CPA and zero conversions (significant spend)</li>
          <li>Identify top converting keywords and increase their bids</li>
          <li>Test landing page changes: headline, CTA, social proof</li>
          <li>Device bid adjustments — check if mobile or desktop is killing CPA</li>
        </ul>
        <div class="plat-signal"><span class="psig" style="background:var(--cb);color:var(--co)">CPA above target</span><span class="psig" style="background:var(--grb);color:var(--gr)">Conversions happening</span></div>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="plat-card">
      <div class="plat-hd"><div class="plat-type" style="color:var(--go)">Plateau Type 3</div><div class="plat-title">Quality Score Plateau — QS Stuck at 5–6</div></div>
      <div class="plat-body">
        <p><strong>Symptoms:</strong> QS not improving, CPC higher than it should be, ad position poor.</p>
        <p><strong>Causes:</strong> Ad copy not mirroring keyword intent, landing page "Average" for experience.</p>
        <ul>
          <li>Identify which QS component is weakest: CTR / Ad Relevance / LP Experience</li>
          <li>Rewrite ad copies to mirror the exact keyword intent in headlines</li>
          <li>Create dedicated ad groups for your top 5 keywords by spend</li>
          <li>Match landing page headline to the keyword that sent the traffic</li>
          <li>Improve page speed — LP experience is partly technical</li>
        </ul>
        <div class="plat-signal"><span class="psig" style="background:var(--gb);color:var(--go)">QS 5–6</span><span class="psig" style="background:var(--rb);color:var(--re)">LP Experience Average</span></div>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="plat-card">
      <div class="plat-hd"><div class="plat-type" style="color:var(--ac)">Plateau Type 4</div><div class="plat-title">Smart Bidding Learning Lock</div></div>
      <div class="plat-body">
        <p><strong>Symptoms:</strong> Performance got worse after switching to tCPA or Maximize Conversions.</p>
        <p><strong>Causes:</strong> Bidding strategy changed before 30+ conversions, or changed twice in quick succession.</p>
        <ul>
          <li>Do NOT switch bidding strategy again — you restart the learning period</li>
          <li>If under 30 conversions: revert to Maximize Conversions (no CPA target)</li>
          <li>If over 30 conversions: set tCPA 20–30% above current CPA initially</li>

      <div class="plat-body">

<section class="sec" id="plateau">
  <div class="sh"><span class="sn">05</span><h2 class="st">Plateaued Campaign Playbooks</h2></div>
  <p class="ss">Six specific plateau types, each with diagnosis signals and exact actions. The wrong fix can reset the learning phase and make things worse &mdash; diagnose first.</p>
  <div class="callout g">A plateau means a campaign has stopped improving despite continued spend. The key is identifying <strong>which type of plateau</strong> before taking action. Wrong diagnosis = wrong fix = wasted spend + reset learning phase. <strong>Universal first step: run the SQR. Budget waste from irrelevant queries causes or masks all 7 plateau types.</strong></div>
  <div class="p6">
    <div class="pcard s">
      <div class="plabel">Plateau Type 1 &mdash; Search</div>
      <div class="ptitle">Impression Share Ceiling</div>
      <p style="font-size:13px;color:var(--tx2);margin-bottom:10px"><strong>Signal:</strong> IS capped at 20&ndash;40% with enough budget but volume won't grow.</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Diagnose:</strong> check IS Lost Due to Rank vs IS Lost Due to Budget &mdash; different fixes</li>
        <li><strong>If rank:</strong> raise max CPC 20% on top QS keywords first</li>
        <li><strong>Expand match types:</strong> add phrase match versions of exact match winners</li>
        <li><strong>New keyword themes:</strong> review SQR for converting long-tail queries to add as exact keywords</li>
        <li><strong>Split ad groups:</strong> move top 5 queries into dedicated ad groups with tailored ad copy</li>
        <li><strong>Add Display retargeting</strong> to capture audiences lost in Search auctions</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="pcard d">
      <div class="plabel">Plateau Type 2 &mdash; Conversion</div>
      <div class="ptitle">CPA Stopped Improving</div>
      <p style="font-size:13px;color:var(--tx2);margin-bottom:10px"><strong>Signal:</strong> Clicks and impressions stable but CPA won't fall below a floor.</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>SQR audit this week:</strong> identify new irrelevant queries burning budget with zero conversions</li>
        <li><strong>Pause high-CPA keywords:</strong> anything spending 3&times; target CPA with zero conversions</li>
        <li><strong>Landing page review:</strong> does ad copy language match the LP headline precisely?</li>
        <li><strong>Switch to Maximise Conversions</strong> if on Manual CPC with 10+ conversions accumulated</li>
        <li><strong>Device bid adjustments:</strong> reduce bids on devices with high clicks but consistently zero conversions</li>
        <li><strong>Ad schedule audit:</strong> identify hours or days with consistent waste pattern</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="pcard g">
      <div class="plabel">Plateau Type 3 &mdash; Quality Score</div>
      <div class="ptitle">QS Stuck at 5&ndash;7</div>
      <p style="font-size:13px;color:var(--tx2);margin-bottom:10px"><strong>Signal:</strong> QS not improving despite conversions. CPC staying high. Ad Strength Average.</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Identify weakest component:</strong> expected CTR, ad relevance, or LP experience? Fix the weakest one first.</li>
        <li><strong>Ad relevance fix:</strong> include the exact keyword text in the first headline</li>
        <li><strong>Expected CTR fix:</strong> add a specific number, question, or urgency to top 3 headlines</li>
        <li><strong>LP experience fix:</strong> ensure LP H1 and meta description match the most common search query</li>
        <li><strong>Tighter ad groups:</strong> move top 5&ndash;10 converting search terms into their own dedicated groups</li>
        <li><strong>Page speed:</strong> slow LP consistently produces Average LP experience score</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="pcard co">
      <div class="plabel">Plateau Type 4 &mdash; Smart Bidding</div>
      <div class="ptitle">Learning Lock / Oscillation</div>
      <p style="font-size:13px;color:var(--tx2);margin-bottom:10px"><strong>Signal:</strong> Campaign enters "learning" repeatedly. CPA spikes then crashes in cycles.</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Stop changing strategies:</strong> every bid strategy change resets the 30-conversion learning clock</li>
        <li><strong>Revert to Maximise Conversions</strong> (no tCPA target) and accumulate 30+ before switching again</li>
        <li><strong>One change rule:</strong> never change bid strategy, budget (more than 15%), and targeting simultaneously</li>
        <li><strong>Wait 2 weeks minimum</strong> after any bid strategy change before evaluating results</li>
        <li><strong>Campaign experiments:</strong> test new bid strategies without resetting the existing campaign's learning</li>
        <li><strong>Verify tracking accuracy</strong> before any smart bidding &mdash; bad data drives bad bid decisions</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="pcard pu">
      <div class="plabel">Plateau Type 5 &mdash; Audience</div>
      <div class="ptitle">Market Saturation / Creative Fatigue</div>
      <p style="font-size:13px;color:var(--tx2);margin-bottom:10px"><strong>Signal:</strong> CTR declining, IS stable, CVR dropping week-over-week. Audience is tired of seeing your ads.</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Frequency cap:</strong> for Display/Demand Gen, set max 3 impressions per user per week</li>
        <li><strong>Refresh creatives:</strong> new ad copy variants, new headlines, new images every 4&ndash;6 weeks minimum</li>
        <li><strong>Expand geography:</strong> city &rarr; country &rarr; region for campaigns with proven local CPA</li>
        <li><strong>New keyword themes:</strong> adjacent intent &mdash; "AI career change", "tech skills 2026", "learn AI online"</li>
        <li><strong>Launch Demand Gen upstream:</strong> reach fresh audiences on YouTube before they search</li>
        <li><strong>RLSA:</strong> add retargeting lists to Search campaigns with bid adjustments for warm audiences</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="pcard gr">
      <div class="plabel">Plateau Type 6 &mdash; PMax</div>
      <div class="ptitle">PMax Spending Without Signal</div>
      <p style="font-size:13px;color:var(--tx2);margin-bottom:10px"><strong>Signal:</strong> PMax consuming large budget share but CPA is significantly above Search campaign CPA.</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Channel performance report:</strong> which channels are spending vs actually converting? (2025 feature)</li>
        <li><strong>Remove low-rated assets:</strong> any asset rated "Low" should be deleted and replaced immediately</li>
        <li><strong>Upload fresh audience signals:</strong> recent customer list, recent converters list (last 30 days)</li>
        <li><strong>Add search themes:</strong> up to 50 per asset group (expanded in 2025) to guide the AI's targeting</li>
        <li><strong>Consider cutting PMax spend:</strong> Grow My Ads Nov 2025 &mdash; cutting 80% PMax spend improved results when Search was stronger. <a href="https://growmyads.com/we-cut-performance-max-spend-by-80-and-still-hit-our-goals/" target="_blank" style="color:var(--gr);font-weight:600">Read the case study &rarr;</a></li>
        <li><strong>Confirm brand exclusions:</strong> brand terms must be negatives in PMax to prevent cannibalisation</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="pcard" style="border-color:var(--br2);background:var(--s)">
      <div class="plabel" style="color:var(--mu)">Plateau Type 7 &mdash; Tracking</div>
      <div class="ptitle">Broken or Miscounted Conversions</div>
      <p style="font-size:13px;color:var(--tx2);margin-bottom:10px"><strong>Signal:</strong> Conversions suddenly dropped or showing as fractions. Google's AI making seemingly random bid decisions.</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>GTM Preview mode:</strong> fire the thank-you page URL manually and check if the conversion tag triggers</li>
        <li><strong>Check URL matching:</strong> GTM trigger URL must exactly match the actual post-form thank-you page URL (trailing slash, https vs http, www vs non-www all matter)</li>
        <li><strong>GA4 DebugView:</strong> watch for the conversion event firing in real time when you submit a test form</li>
        <li><strong>Data-driven attribution:</strong> fractional conversions (6.86 instead of 7) are normal — it's DDA splitting credit across touchpoints, not a tracking error</li>
        <li><strong>Cross-campaign attribution:</strong> if Search and PMax run simultaneously, DDA can split a single conversion between them — both show partial credit</li>
        <li><strong>Primary vs secondary conversions:</strong> ensure only one primary conversion action per campaign goal — multiple primary actions inflate reported conversions</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>

  document.getElementById('qct').textContent=m.name+' \u2014 Q'+(curQ+1)+' of 5';

  document.getElementById('qpf').style.width=(curQ/5*100)+'%';

  });

function selA(idx,el){
  if(answered)return;
  answered=true;
  const q=MODS[curMod].qs[curQ];
  document.querySelectorAll('.qopt').forEach(o=>o.classList.add('ans'));
  if(idx===q.a){
    el.classList.add('right');
    modScore++;
    document.getElementById('qhint').style.color='var(--gr)';
    document.getElementById('qhint').textContent='\u2713 Correct!';
  } else {
    el.classList.add('wrng');
    document.querySelectorAll('.qopt')[q.a].classList.add('show-r');
    document.getElementById('qhint').style.color='var(--rd)';
    document.getElementById('qhint').textContent='\u2717 Not quite \u2014 see explanation below';
  }
  const e=document.getElementById('qexpl');
  e.innerHTML=q.e;
  e.className='qexpl on';
  document.getElementById('qsc').textContent='Score: '+modScore+'/5';
  document.getElementById('qnxt').disabled=false;
}
