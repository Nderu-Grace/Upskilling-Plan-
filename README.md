  <div class="section-header"><span class="section-num">01</span><h2 class="section-title">Glossary — Every Acronym Decoded</h2></div>
  <p class="section-sub">Learn these cold before anything else. You will see every one of these in your Google Ads account daily.</p>
  <div class="gloss-grid">
    <div class="gloss-card"><div class="gloss-abbr ca">CPC</div><div class="gloss-full">Cost Per Click</div><div class="gloss-def">What you pay each time someone clicks your ad. Formula: total spend ÷ clicks. The most basic bidding unit in Search and Display campaigns.</div></div>
    <div class="gloss-card"><div class="gloss-abbr cg">CPA</div><div class="gloss-full">Cost Per Acquisition</div><div class="gloss-def">What you pay for one conversion — a lead, sign-up, or sale. Formula: spend ÷ conversions. Moringa's current CPA is $42–$103. Target should be $15–$30.</div></div>
    <div class="gloss-card"><div class="gloss-abbr ct">CPL</div><div class="gloss-full">Cost Per Lead</div><div class="gloss-def">Same calculation as CPA but specifically for leads (form fills, sign-ups, phone calls). This is Moringa's primary success metric for course campaigns.</div></div>
    <div class="gloss-card"><div class="gloss-abbr cc">CPM</div><div class="gloss-full">Cost Per Thousand Impressions</div><div class="gloss-def">What you pay per 1,000 times your ad is shown. Used in Display and Demand Gen when the goal is brand awareness, not direct clicks.</div></div>
    <div class="gloss-card"><div class="gloss-abbr cgr">CTR</div><div class="gloss-full">Click-Through Rate</div><div class="gloss-def">Percentage of people who saw your ad and clicked it. Formula: clicks ÷ impressions × 100. Strong CTR signals relevance and directly improves Quality Score.</div></div>
    <div class="gloss-card"><div class="gloss-abbr ca">CVR</div><div class="gloss-full">Conversion Rate</div><div class="gloss-def">Percentage of clicks that become a lead or sale. Formula: conversions ÷ clicks × 100. Education benchmark: 2–5%. Moringa is currently at 0.47–1.0%.</div></div>
    <div class="gloss-card"><div class="gloss-abbr cg">ROAS</div><div class="gloss-full">Return On Ad Spend</div><div class="gloss-def">Revenue generated per dollar spent. $5 ROAS = every $1 in ads returned $5 in revenue. More relevant for e-commerce with varying order values than course lead-gen.</div></div>
    <div class="gloss-card"><div class="gloss-abbr ct">QS</div><div class="gloss-full">Quality Score</div><div class="gloss-def">Google's 1–10 score per keyword. Based on expected CTR, ad relevance, and landing page experience. Higher QS = lower CPC and better ad position. Moringa's best keywords: QS 8.</div></div>
    <div class="gloss-card"><div class="gloss-abbr cc">IS</div><div class="gloss-full">Impression Share</div><div class="gloss-def">Percentage of eligible auctions where your ad actually appeared. Moringa's IS is 10–14% — 86% of opportunities lost, mostly due to the $0.01 max CPC bid.</div></div>
    <div class="gloss-card"><div class="gloss-abbr cgr">RSA</div><div class="gloss-full">Responsive Search Ad</div><div class="gloss-def">The standard Search ad format. You provide up to 15 headlines and 4 descriptions. Google's AI tests combinations to find the best performing version for each search.</div></div>
    <div class="gloss-card"><div class="gloss-abbr ca">tCPA</div><div class="gloss-full">Target CPA Bidding</div><div class="gloss-def">A smart bidding strategy — you set your desired cost per conversion and Google adjusts bids automatically to hit it. Needs 30+ conversions first to work properly.</div></div>
    <div class="gloss-card"><div class="gloss-abbr cg">tROAS</div><div class="gloss-full">Target ROAS Bidding</div><div class="gloss-def">You tell Google your target return on ad spend; it bids accordingly. More useful for e-commerce with varying revenue values than fixed-price course lead generation.</div></div>
    <div class="gloss-card"><div class="gloss-abbr ct">PMax</div><div class="gloss-full">Performance Max</div><div class="gloss-def">Google's fully automated campaign type across all channels — Search, Display, YouTube, Gmail, Maps, and Discover — from one campaign using Google's AI.</div></div>
    <div class="gloss-card"><div class="gloss-abbr cc">Demand Gen</div><div class="gloss-full">Demand Generation</div><div class="gloss-def">Google's visual/video-first campaign type on YouTube, YouTube Shorts, Gmail, and Discover. Replaced Video Action Campaigns in 2025. Creates demand before people search.</div></div>

<section class="section" id="analogy">
  <div class="section-header"><span class="section-num">03</span><h2 class="section-title">The Restaurant Analogy — When to Launch Each Campaign</h2></div>
  <p class="section-sub">Think of your Google Ads account as opening a restaurant. Each campaign type is a stage of growing that business. You don't run a city-wide TV ad before you've proven the kitchen works.</p>
  <div class="analogy-card s"><div class="a-label">Phase 1 — Search Campaigns</div><h3>Your restaurant opens its front door</h3><p>Search is <strong>serving customers already standing outside, wallet in hand, reading your menu.</strong> They typed "AI marketing course Nairobi" — they have intent, they have money, they just need to choose you. This is your most efficient spend. You don't put up a billboard across town (Display) or run a TV commercial (Demand Gen) before you've proven the kitchen produces food people want to buy. <strong>Start here. Prove your CPA. Fix conversion tracking. Find your winning keywords.</strong></p></div>
  <div class="analogy-card d"><div class="a-label">Phase 2 — Display Campaigns</div><h3>A loyalty card for guests who peeked in and walked out</h3><p>Display is <strong>the loyalty card you hand to someone who came in, looked at the menu, then left without ordering.</strong> Retargeting via Display follows those people across the web with your ad for the next 30 days. Someone visited the AI Agents landing page, looked at fees, left — Display brings them back for a fraction of the cost of re-acquiring them through Search. <strong>Launch Display after Search has conversions and you have 500+ site visitors in your retargeting audience.</strong></p></div>
  <div class="analogy-card dg"><div class="a-label">Phase 3 — Demand Gen Campaigns</div><h3>A viral short video that makes people hungry before they knew they were</h3><p>Demand Gen <strong>creates hunger in people who weren't thinking about eating.</strong> They're scrolling YouTube Shorts watching tech content — your 30-second ad shows a career transformation story after completing your AI course. They didn't search for you. But now they will. This builds the pipeline that eventually converts through Search. <strong>Launch Demand Gen when Search CPA is proven, video assets exist, and you want to grow total lead volume rather than just efficiency.</strong></p></div>
  <div class="analogy-card pm"><div class="a-label">Phase 4 — Performance Max</div><h3>Hiring a world-class maître d' to run the whole operation</h3><p>PMax is <strong>giving Google full control of your dining room, kitchen, and delivery service simultaneously.</strong> It's powerful — but the maître d' needs training data. Google needs at least 30 conversions to learn who your best customers are. Give PMax control before Search is working and it will spend your budget blindly across all channels with no signal to guide it. <strong>Launch PMax only after 30+ conversions in 30 days, all creative assets are ready, and brand terms are excluded as negatives.</strong></p></div>
  <div class="callout g"><strong>The sequencing rule:</strong> Search → Display (retargeting) → Demand Gen (pipeline) → PMax (scale). Each step requires the previous one to be proven with real conversion data. For Moringa in May 2026: you are in Stage 1. Fix Search first — everything else must wait.</div>
</section>
